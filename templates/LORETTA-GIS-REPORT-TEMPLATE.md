## 1. What this packet is

- Nine GIS sheets (G-001 cover through 3D-3 grading exhibit) plus a separate Tentative Tract Map (TTM 39456, Cannon Engineering) and HELIX biological/jurisdictional figures.
- **Every GIS sheet carries the same limit:** desktop screening only — NOT a survey, geotechnical investigation, or engineered grading/drainage plan.
- HELIX jurisdictional and vegetation figures are reproduced unchanged and were not field-checked. The report itself says so.

## 3. Cover sheet (G-001) — required elements

- **Title block:** project name, location (cross streets / area plan), APNs, area, CRS, datum, date, prepared-for, sheet number.
- **FEMA Zone AE callout:** acres and % of GIS area, note that blue fill is clipped to the parcels, and whether a base flood elevation (BFE) is assigned. If no BFE: state it explicitly.
- **Consolidated findings sidebar:** terrain (elevation range, relief, mean slope), earthwork material correction, FEMA/independent overlay, soils summary, planning status (zoning, GPA status, yield), HELIX/source figures note, sheet index, basis/companion utility exhibits note.
- **Four-parcel / two-area-bases box:** recorded acres vs GIS analysis acres vs any tract-map gross acres — quote all three and say which basis each number uses.
- **Disclaimer footer:** DESKTOP GIS SCREENING — NOT A SURVEY, GEOTECHNICAL INVESTIGATION OR ENGINEERED GRADING / DRAINAGE PLAN.

## 4. Terrain (EX-1) — required elements

- USGS 3DEP 1 m DEM (or better), acquisition dates, publication date.
- 2 ft index / 10 ft intermediate contours; sampled high and low with locations.
- Terrain summary table: analysis area, min/max/mean elevation, total relief, mean slope (Horn 3x3), area with slope >15%, area with slope >25%.
- Method/data-date box and sources/interpretation box (DEM is a terrain screen — not runoff, flow-routing, outlet-capacity, or an engineered drainage model).
- Note: contour interval is not a vertical-accuracy statement.

## 5. Earthwork (GR-1) — required elements

- Illustrative earthwork map: horizontal comparison plane, fill/cut depth classes (fill >8, >2, >0.5; near zero; cut 0.5–2, 2–8, >8), black dashed zero-cut/fill line, subject-parcel outline.
- **Audit correction box (mandatory when volumes are reported):** recompute cut/fill on the opposite drainage direction. A near-identical volume on the reversed slope is the signature of a **slope sign reversal**, not a real volume — flag it and publish the corrected figure.
- Illustrative quantities table: geometric cut, geometric fill, geometric fill space, 12% shrink shortfall (CCY), import (BCY) — with the yield assumption stated (e.g., 0.88 bank-to-compacted, unverified) and source (USGS 1 m DEM + assessor parcel geometry, fractional boundary-cell weights).
- Whole-site sensitivity note: no environmental exclusions or engineered grading tie-ins applied; the map does not establish a feasible grading footprint.
- Max geometric cut/fill depths labeled; note these require engineering review.

## 6. Jurisdictional features (HELIX Fig. 5) — required elements

- USACE/RWQCB jurisdictional waters (blue) and wetlands (orange), CDFW/MSHCP riparian riverine (green), presumed connections (dashed), culverts, potential pools.
- Man-made ditch / outfall callouts where present.
- Source and date (aerial).
- Explicit statement: desktop audit does not establish agency concurrence; a formal delineation and agency verification are required before yield.

## 7. Soils (SO-1) — required elements

- USDA NRCS SSURGO map units with code, name, slope range, acres, dominant hydrologic soil group (HSG).
- Drainage/farmland interpretation: HSG D totals, poorly drained acreage, NRCS farmland ratings (statewide/prime if irrigated) with the caveat that ratings do not establish FMMP designation or Williamson Act enrollment.
- Source: USDA NRCS Soil Data Access query date.

## 8. Vegetation (HELIX Fig. 4) — required elements

- Vegetation/land-use classes with sensitive communities flagged per CDFW Natural Community List (asterisk).
- Source and date.

## 9. 3D exhibits (3D-1 / 3D-2 / 3D-3) — required elements

- Perspective view labeled NOT TO SCALE; vertical exaggeration stated (3x typical).
- Frontage/dimensions from GIS geometry (not a survey) with the caveat.
- 3D-2: elevation tint + hillshade, FEMA AE overlay, legend with elevation range of the block.
- 3D-3: finish-grade (FG) surface for the chosen scenario, cut/fill labels at extrema, FG formula (e.g., FG = mean + slope coefficient × (northing − mean northing)), max cut/fill callouts.
- Notes/sources box: DEM, imagery, datum, vertical exaggeration, and the desktop-screening disclaimer.

## 10. Acreage discipline (mandatory)

Always present three numbers when they differ:

1. **Recorded acres** (assessor geometry).
2. **GIS analysis acres** (fractional boundary cells).
3. **Tract-map gross / net acres** (if a TTM exists).

State which basis each quoted figure uses. Never mix them in one sentence without labeling.

## 11. Disclaimers (carry on every sheet)

- DESKTOP GIS SCREENING — NOT A SURVEY, GEOTECHNICAL INVESTIGATION OR ENGINEERED GRADING / DRAINAGE PLAN.
- HELIX figures retain original content; this desktop audit does not independently verify field delineations or establish agency concurrence.
- 3D views are perspective, not to scale; vertical exaggeration applies.
- Frontage/dimensions from GIS geometry — not a survey.

## 12. What this template does NOT include (gaps to close on future projects)

- Utilities (power, water, sewer, gas POCs) — companion EX-2 utility sheets.
- Geotech borings / fault / liquefaction screening.
- Flood study with BFE; CLOMR/LOMR path.
- Formal jurisdictional delineation concurrence.
- Entitlement status verification beyond what the county confirms in writing.
- Grading takeoff including strip, shrink, walls, and the flood spine.
