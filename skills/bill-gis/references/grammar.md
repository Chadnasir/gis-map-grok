# Bill GIS — map grammar

Copy published sheet language. Do not invent a new style.

## Assessor

Book/page, lot numbers in circles, recorded dimensions on lines, street widths, TRA, tract number. No owner names. No situs addresses on the plat.

## Consultant study exhibit (GLA / Cadre / Urban Crossroads)

Firm logo, project name, exhibit number, State Plane 6 NAD83, Lambert Conformal Conic, cartographer, date, MXD path, north arrow, graphic scale, acreage table.

Typical bio stack — regional, vicinity, site/impact, MSHCP overlay or BUOW hatch, vegetation communities, soils (SSURGO units), jurisdictional / riverine lines.

Typical TIA stack — location, site plan, numbered study intersections, existing lanes and control, GP circulation, existing ADT, car trip distribution, truck trip distribution, opening-year and cumulative volumes. Truck PCE 2-axle 1.5, 3-axle 2.0, 4-axle 3.0.

## MSHCP

Square criteria cells with unique IDs. Survey-area hatches. Conserved lands as polygons. Not a habitat radius.

## CEQA figure stack

Regional location. Vicinity / USGS quad. Site on imagery. Existing land use. GP + zoning. Natural communities. Vegetation. Soils. Jurisdictional waters. Traffic study area. Noise receivers. Flood 100/500. Alquist-Priolo / liquefaction / slope. Cumulative projects. One theme per sheet.

## Density

Census tract or block-group choropleth (people per square mile). Not a heat blob or ring.

## Industrial traffic

Official truck-route lines plus warehouse footprints plus truck ADT. AB 98 maps put routes on industrial streets and show sensitive-receptor buffers as mapped bands, not as a circle around the building.
